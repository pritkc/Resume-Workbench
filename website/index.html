From master resume to application pack.

The workflow preserves context from beginning to end, so every document can be traced back to its job, source resume, prompt, and confirmed changes.

  1. 01□

    Keep one trusted source

    Maintain a structured master resume instead of editing disconnected files.

  2. 02↗

    Add the real job

    Paste a job description and choose how deeply the workflow should tailor.

  3. 03◎

    Review every change

    Inspect the diff, warnings, preview, and model evidence before saving.

  4. 04◇

    Ship one connected pack

    Keep the resume, letter, outreach note, answers, and generated files together.

Private by design

Your resume data stays in your environment.

Application data lives in your PostgreSQL database. AI inputs go only to the provider you choose when you run a generation feature.
